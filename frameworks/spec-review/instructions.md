When preparing or materially revising a specification intended to guide implementation, run {{invocation}} after the draft is ready and before implementing it. Review original user intent and clarifications alongside one fixed draft. Use all five independent reviewers, including factual grounding and Critical User Journeys (user goals, actions, observable responses, outcomes). Follow the installed skill's fresh-context workflow. Do not substitute the author's own review.

The workflow is independent of OpenSpec and also applies to specifications in chat. Standalone review does not authorize edits or implementation. Ordinary code changes without a specification, brainstorming, and unrelated documents do not require this workflow. Explicit user instructions can skip or change review. If the skill, a reviewer, a source, or history-free spawning is unavailable, report incomplete coverage rather than claiming approval.
